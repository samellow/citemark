# Text emphasis

In Zulip, you can make text bold or italic, or cross it out with strikethrough.

## Making text bold

**Via compose box button:**

1. Open the compose box.
2. Select the text you want to format.
3. Click the **bold** () icon at the bottom of the compose box.

 **Keyboard tip:** You can also use `Ctrl` + `B` to insert bold formatting.

**Via Markdown:**

1. Open the compose box.
2. To make text bold, use double asterisks (`**`) around the text: `**text**`.

 **Keyboard tip:** You can also use `Ctrl` + `B` to insert bold formatting.

## Making text italic

**Via compose box button:**

1. Open the compose box.
2. Select the text you want to format.
3. Click the **italic** () icon at the bottom of the compose box.

 **Keyboard tip:** You can also use `Ctrl` + `I` to insert italic formatting.

**Via Markdown:**

1. Open the compose box.
2. To make text italic, use single asterisks (`*`) around the text: `*text*`.

 **Keyboard tip:** You can also use `Ctrl` + `I` to insert italic formatting.

## Applying strikethrough formatting

**Via compose box button:**

1. Open the compose box.
2. Select the text you want to format.
3. Click the **strikethrough** () icon at the bottom
of the compose box.

**Via Markdown:**

1. Open the compose box.
2. To apply strikethrough formatting, use two tildes (`~~`) around the text: `~~text~~`.

## Examples

### What you type

```
**bold**, *italic*, and ~~strikethrough~~ text
***~~All three at once~~***
```
