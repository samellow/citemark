# View someone's profile

A user’s profile displays key information about the user.

## Information in a user’s profile

**Desktop/Web:**

- Their name.
- Their availability.
- Their profile picture.
- Their email address, if you have permission to view it.
- Their user ID.
- Their role in the organization.
- The date they joined the organization.
- Their current local time.
- Any custom profile fields they’ve filled out.

Additional tabs showing:

- The channels that the user is subscribed to. Note that the list is limited to channels for which you have permission to see all subscribers.
- The user groups to which they belong.

**Mobile:**

- Their name.
- Their availability and status.
- Their email address, if you have permission to view it.
- Their role in the organization.
- Any custom profile fields they’ve filled out.

## View someone’s profile

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Hover over a user’s name in the right sidebar.
2. Click on their avatar or the **ellipsis** () to the right of their name to open their **user card**.
3. Click **View profile**.

 **Tip:** You can also click on a user’s profile picture or name on a
message they sent to open their **user card**, and skip to
the last step.

**Mobile:**

1. Tap on a user’s profile picture or name.

## Share someone’s profile

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Hover over a user’s name in the right sidebar.
2. Click on their avatar or the **ellipsis** () to the right of their name to open their **user card**.
3. Click **View profile**.
4. Click on the **link** ()
icon to the right of their name to copy the URL for their profile.
