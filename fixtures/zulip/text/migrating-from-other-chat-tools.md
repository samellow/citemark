# Migrating from other chat tools

If your organization is moving to Zulip from another chat tool, follow the dedicated guide to get started.

- Moving from **Slack**. You can import your Slack
workspace. Zulip’s Slack-compatible incoming
webhook also makes it easy
to migrate any integrations.
- Moving from **Microsoft Teams**.
- Moving from **Discord**.
- Import from **Mattermost**, then follow the
general guide on moving to Zulip.
- Import from **Rocket.Chat**, then follow the
general guide on moving to Zulip.
- For all other tools, follow the general guide on moving to Zulip.

**Note:**

You can import data from Slack, Mattermost, and Rocket.Chat. **You can only
import a workspace as a new Zulip organization.** Your imported message history
cannot be added into an existing Zulip organization.
