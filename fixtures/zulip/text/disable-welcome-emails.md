# Disable welcome emails

**Note:**

This feature is only available to organization owners and administrators.

Zulip sends a handful of emails to new users, introducing them to the Zulip app, when they join an organization. If these emails don’t make sense for your organization, you can disable them.

Note that regardless of this setting, users will receive an initial account email notifying them about their new Zulip account and how to log in.

## Disable welcome emails

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select  **Organization settings**.
3. On the left, click **Organization settings**.
4. Under **Onboarding**, toggle **Send emails introducing Zulip to new users**.
