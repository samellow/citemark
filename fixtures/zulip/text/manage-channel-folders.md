# Manage channel folders

Organizations can sort channels into folders. For example, you can put all the
channels associated with a team into a dedicated folder. Users can decide
whether to group channels by folder in the **Inbox** view and
the left sidebar.

Everyone can pin channels they personally want to pay
close attention to. Pinned channels appear in a dedicated **pinned channels**
section above channel folders.

Folders that only contain inactive and muted channels are sorted at the bottom.

## Move a channel to a folder

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select  **Channel settings**.
3. Click **All** in the upper left.
4. Select a channel.
5. Select the **General** tab on the right.
6. *(optional)* Create a new channel folder.
7. Under **Settings**, select the desired folder from the **Channel folder** dropdown.
8. Click **Save changes**.

 **Tip:** You can also click on a channel name in the navigation bar at the top of the
app to access its settings, or use the **ellipsis** ()
next to the channel name in the left sidebar.

## Create a new channel folder

**Note:**

This feature is only available to organization owners and administrators.

**Via organization settings:**

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select  **Organization settings**.
3. On the left, click **Channel folders**.
4. Click **Add a new channel folder**.
5. Fill out channel folder information as desired, and click **Create**.
6. In the **Name** column, click and drag the vertical dots to reorder channel
folders.

**Via channel settings:**

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select  **Channel settings**.
3. Click **All** in the upper left.
4. Select any channel.
5. Select the **General** tab on the right.
6. Under **Settings**, click the **Create new folder** button to the right
of the folder selection dropdown.
7. Fill out the requested information, and click **Create**.

## Manage a channel folder

**Note:**

This feature is only available to organization owners and administrators.

**Via organization settings:**

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select  **Organization settings**.
3. On the left, click **Channel folders**.
4. In the **Actions** column, click the **manage folder** ()
icon for the channel folder you want to manage.
5. Edit channel folder information as desired, and click **Save changes**.

**Via channel settings:**

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select  **Channel settings**.
3. Click **All** in the upper left.
4. Select any channel.
5. Select the **General** tab on the right.
6. Under **Settings**, click the **manage folder** () icon
for the channel folder you want to edit in the **Channel folder** dropdown.
7. Edit channel folder information as desired, and click **Save changes**.

## Delete a channel folder

**Note:**

This feature is only available to organization owners and administrators.

**Via organization settings:**

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select  **Organization settings**.
3. On the left, click **Channel folders**.
4. In the **Actions** column, click the **delete** () icon
for the channel folder you want to delete.
5. Approve by clicking **Confirm**.

**Via channel settings:**

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select  **Channel settings**.
3. Click **All** in the upper left.
4. Select any channel.
5. Select the **General** tab on the right.
6. Under **Settings**, click the **delete** () icon for the
channel folder you want to delete in the **Channel folder** dropdown.
7. Approve by clicking **Confirm**.

## Reorder channel folders

**Note:**

This feature is only available to organization owners and administrators.

Users will see channel folders in the specified order.

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select  **Organization settings**.
3. On the left, click **Channel folders**.
4. In the **Name** column, click and drag the vertical dots to reorder channel
folders.
