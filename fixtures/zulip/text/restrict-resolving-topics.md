# Restrict resolving topics

Zulip lets you configure who can mark topics as resolved. This permission can be granted to any combination of roles, groups, and individual users.

## Configure who can resolve topics in any channel

**Note:**

This feature is only available to organization owners and administrators.

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select  **Organization settings**.
3. On the left, click **Organization permissions**.
4. Under **Moving messages**, configure **Who can resolve topics**.
5. Click **Save changes**.

## Configure who can resolve topics in a specific channel

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select  **Channel settings**.
3. Click **All** in the upper left.
4. Select a channel.
5. Select the **Permissions** tab on the right.
6. Under **Moderation permissions**, configure **Who can resolve topics in this
channel**.
7. Click **Save changes**.

 **Tip:** You can also click on a channel name in the navigation bar at the top of the
app to access its settings, or use the **ellipsis** ()
next to the channel name in the left sidebar.
