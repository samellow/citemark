# Configure email visibility

With privacy in mind, Zulip lets you control which roles in the organization can view your email address. Administrators can set the default email visibility configuration for new users in the organization.

## Configure who can access your email address

**On sign-up:**

1. After confirming your email, click **Change** below your email address.
2. Configure **Who can access your email address**.
3. Click **Confirm** to apply your changes, and continue the account registration
process.

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select  **Personal settings**.
3. On the left, click **Account & privacy**.
4. Under **Privacy**, configure **Who can access your email address**.

## Configure default email visibility for new users

**Note:**

This feature is only available to organization owners and administrators.

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select  **Organization settings**.
3. On the left, click **Default user settings**.
4. Under **Privacy settings**, configure **Who can access user’s email address**.
