# Status and availability

Status and availability let everyone know roughly how quickly you’ll be responding to messages.

A **status** is a customizable emoji, along with a short message. A typical
status might be “📅 In a meeting” or “🏠 Working remotely”. To make it easy to
notice, the status emoji is shown next to your name in the sidebars, message
feed, and compose box.

Your **availability** is a colored dot (like ) that indicates if you’re currently active on Zulip, idle,
or offline. You can also go invisible to appear offline
to other users.

## Statuses

### Set a status

You can set a status emoji, status message, or both.

**Via user card:**

1. Hover over your name in the right sidebar.
2. Click on the **ellipsis** ()
to open your **user card**.
3. Click **Set status** or **Edit status**.
4. Click to select one of the common statuses, *or* choose any emoji and/or
write a short message.
5. Click **Save**.

 **Keyboard tip:** You can also use `Shift` + `Y` to open the status menu.

**Via personal settings:**

1. Click on your **profile picture** in the upper right
corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Click **Set status** or **Edit status**.
3. Click to select one of the common statuses, *or* choose any emoji and/or
write a short message.
4. Click **Save**.

 **Keyboard tip:** You can also use `Shift` + `Y` to open the status menu.

**Mobile:**

1. Tap the **menu** () tab in the bottom right corner of the app.
2. Tap **My profile**.
3. Tap **Set status** or **Status**.
4. Select one of the common statuses, or choose any emoji and/or write a short message.
5. Tap **Save**.

**Via user card:**

1. Hover over your name in the right sidebar.
2. Click on the **ellipsis** ()

 **Keyboard tip:** You can also use `Shift` + `Y` to open the status menu.

**Via personal settings:**

1. Click on your **profile picture** in the upper right

2. Click on the to the right of your current status.

 **Keyboard tip:** You can also use `Shift` + `Y` to open the status menu.

### View a status

Status emoji are shown next to a user’s name in the sidebars, message feed, and compose box in the web and desktop apps, and next to the user’s profile picture and name in the mobile app.

Status emoji and status messages are also shown on user cards in the web and desktop apps.

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Hover over a user’s name in the right sidebar, or in the **Direct messages** section in the left sidebar, to view their status message if they have one
set.

 **Tip:** You can also click on a user’s profile picture or name on a message they
sent to view their status in their **user card**, or configure status text
to always be shown in the right sidebar.

**Mobile:**

1. Tap on a user’s profile picture or name to view their status message.
2. If they have one set, it will appear under their name on their profile.

### Configure how statuses are displayed

You can choose whether or not status text is displayed in the right sidebar. With the compact option, only status emoji are shown.

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select  **Personal settings**.
3. On the left, click **Preferences**.
4. Under **Information**, select **Compact** or **Show status and text** for the
user list style.

 **Tip:** You can always hover over a user’s name in the right sidebar to view their
status message if they have one set.

## Availability

There are three availability states:

- **Active** (): Zulip is open and in focus on web,
desktop or mobile, or was in the last 140 seconds.
- **Idle** (): Zulip is open on your computer (either
desktop or web), but you are not active.
- **Offline** (): Zulip is not open on your computer,
or you have turned on invisible mode.

### View availability

**Desktop/Web:**

1. View a user’s availability on the user list in the right sidebar, the direct messages list in the left sidebar, or their user card. If there is no availability indicator, the user is offline.

 **Tip:** You can see when someone offline was last active by hovering over their
name in the left or right sidebar.

**Mobile:**

1. Tap on a user’s profile picture or name. Their availability appears to the left of their name on their profile.

### Invisible mode

Zulip supports the privacy option of never updating the availability
information for your account. The result is that you will always
appear to other users as **Offline**, regardless of your activity in
Zulip.

With this setting, your “Last active” time displayed to other users in the UI will be frozen as the approximate time you enabled this setting. Your activity will still be included in your organization’s statistics.

 **Tip:** Consider also not
allowing
other users to see when you have read messages.

### Toggle invisible mode

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Hover over your name in the right sidebar.
2. Click on the **ellipsis** ()
to open your **user card**.
3. To enable, you’ll select **Go invisible**.
4. To disable, you’ll select **Turn off invisible mode**.

 **Tip:** You can also toggle this setting in the **Account & privacy**
tab of your **Personal settings** menu.

**Mobile:**

1. Tap the **menu** () tab in the bottom right corner of the app.
2. Tap **My profile**.
3. Toggle **Invisible mode**.
