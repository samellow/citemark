# Schedule a message

Zulip lets you schedule a message to be sent at a later time. For example, if you are working outside of regular business hours for your organization, you can schedule a message for next morning to avoid disturbing others. You can also schedule a message reminder for yourself.

## Schedule a message

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Open the compose box.
2. Write a message.
3. Click the **ellipsis** () next to the **send** () button.
4. Click **Schedule message**.
5. Select one of the suggested scheduling options, or pick a custom time.

 **Keyboard tip:** From the compose box, you can use `Tab`, `Tab`,
`Enter` to open the compose menu and start scheduling a message.

## Edit or reschedule a message

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Click on  **Scheduled messages** in the left sidebar. If the **views** section is collapsed, click on
the **ellipsis** (), and
select **Scheduled messages**.
2. Click on the message you want to edit or reschedule.
3. *(optional)* Edit the message.
4. Click the **ellipsis** () next to the **send** () button.
5. Select the previously scheduled time, or click **Schedule message** to pick a new time.

 **Tip:** You can also click **Undo** in the confirmation banner shown immediately
after a message is scheduled.

## Send a scheduled message now

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Click on  **Scheduled messages** in the left sidebar. If the **views** section is collapsed, click on
the **ellipsis** (), and
select **Scheduled messages**.
2. Click on the message you want to send now.
3. *(optional)* Edit the message.
4. Click the **send** () button, or use a keyboard
shortcut to send your message.

 **Keyboard tip:** You can also use `Enter` within the scheduled messages view to
edit or reschedule the selected message.

## Delete a scheduled message

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Click on  **Scheduled messages** in the left sidebar. If the **views** section is collapsed, click on
the **ellipsis** (), and
select **Scheduled messages**.
2. Click the **delete** () icon on the message you
want to delete.

 **Keyboard tip:** You can also use `Backspace` within the scheduled messages view to
delete the selected message.

## View scheduled messages

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Click on  **Scheduled messages** in the left sidebar. If the **views** section is collapsed, click on
the **ellipsis** (), and
select **Scheduled messages**.

 **Tip:** You can also view scheduled messages by clicking on the **ellipsis**
() next to the **send** () button in
the compose box, and selecting **View scheduled messages**.
