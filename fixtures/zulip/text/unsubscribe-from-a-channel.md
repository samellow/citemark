# Unsubscribe from a channel

You can always unsubscribe from any channel in Zulip. Unsubscribing from a channel removes conversations in that channel from your inbox, recent conversations, and left sidebar. New messages will not appear in your combined feed, and you will not receive @-mention notifications in channels you’re not subscribed to. The app doesn’t track your unread messages in unsubscribed channels.

 **Tip:** Unsubscribe from channels where you don’t plan to read the conversations. You
can still follow a link to a specific
conversation
in any channel you have content access to.

Alternatively, you can mute channels to avoid receiving notifications for messages you are not interested in.

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Hover over a channel in the left sidebar.
2. Click on the **ellipsis** ().
3. Click **Unsubscribe**.

**Mobile:**

1. Tap the **channels** () tab at the bottom of the app.
2. Press and hold a channel until the long-press menu appears.
3. Tap **Unsubscribe**.

## Alternate methods to unsubscribe from a channel

### Via subscribed channels

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select **Channel settings**.
3. Click the **unsubscribe from channel** ()
icon to the left of a channel to unsubscribe from it.

### Via channel settings

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Hover over a channel in the left sidebar.
2. Click on the **ellipsis** ().
3. Click **Channel settings**.
4. Click **Unsubscribe** near the top right corner of the channel settings panel.

 **Keyboard tip:** You can also use `Shift` + `S` to unsubscribe from the
selected channel.
