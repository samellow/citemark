# Follow a topic

Zulip lets you follow topics you are interested in, which helps you see when you have unread messages you care about. You can follow or unfollow any topic, and choose which topics you’d like to follow automatically:

- Topics you start.
- Topics you send a message to.
- Topics you participate in by sending a message, reacting with an emoji, or responding to a poll.
- Topics where you are mentioned.

Followed topics are marked with in the left sidebar, inbox, and elsewhere. It’s easy to prioritize catching up on followed topics. You can:

- Configure how you get notified about new messages for topics you follow.
- Use the `Shift` + `N` keyboard
shortcut to go to the next unread followed topic.
- Filter to view only followed topics in the inbox, recent conversations, and list of topics in a channel.
- Filter to view only followed topics when viewing all topics in a channel in the left sidebar.

You can use followed topics for a variety of workflows:

- Catch up on followed topics at the start of the day, and whenever you want to spend a few minutes checking on the conversations that need your attention.
- No more stressing about missing a reply to your message in channels you don’t regularly read. You can automatically follow topics you start or participate in.
- You can also mute the channels you don’t regularly read, and automatically follow or unmute topics you start or participate in. In muted channels, topics you follow are automatically treated as unmuted, so it will be easy to see when someone responds to your message.
- If you like, follow just the topics where your prompt attention is needed, and enable desktop and mobile notifications for followed topics.

## Follow or unfollow a topic

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Hover over a topic in the left sidebar.
2. Click on the **ellipsis** ().
3. Configure topic notifications using the row of icons at the top of the menu.

 **Tip:** You can also configure notifications by clicking the topic notifications
status icon ( for mute,
 for default,
 for unmute, or
 for follow) wherever it appears.

**Mobile:**

1. Press and hold a topic until the long-press menu appears.
2. Tap **Follow topic** or **Unfollow topic**.

## Catch up on followed topics

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Click on  **Inbox** (or  if the **views** section is collapsed) in the left sidebar,
or use the `Shift` + `I` keyboard shortcut.
2. Filter the view to the topics you follow by selecting **Followed topics** from the dropdown in the upper left of the **inbox** view.
3. Click on a conversation you’re interested in to view it. You can also use
the arrow keys to select a conversation, and press `Enter`.
4. Return to **Inbox** when done to select the next conversation. You can use
the **back** button in your browser or the desktop app, `Shift` + `I`, or `Esc` if **Inbox** is configured as you home
view.

 **Keyboard tip:** You can also use `Shift` + `N` from any location to go
to the next unread followed topic.

## Search for messages in followed topics

**Search bar:**

1. Click the **search** () icon in the top bar to open the search box.
2. Type `is:followed`, or start typing and select **Followed topics** from the
typeahead.
3. *(optional)* Enter additional search terms or
filters.
4. Press `Enter`.

 **Keyboard tip:** You can also use the `/` or `Ctrl` + `K`
keyboard shortcut to start searching messages.

 **Tip:** To get a feed of unread messages in all the topics you follow, search for
`is:followed is:unread`.

**Left sidebar:**

1. Click on a channel in the left sidebar.
2. Click **show all topics** at the bottom of the list of recent topics in the
selected channel.
3. In the search box at the top of the list of topics, type `is:`.
4. Choose **followed** from the typeahead suggestions.

## Configure notifications for followed topics

You can configure custom notifications for followed topics. You can also configure the unread count badge to include unread messages just in followed topics, without counting other channel messages.

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select  **Personal settings**.
3. On the left, click **Notifications**.
4. In the **Notification triggers** table,
toggle the settings for **Followed topics**.

 **Tip:** You will receive both followed topics notifications and
channel notifications in
topics you follow.

## Automatically follow topics

### Follow topics you start or participate in

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select  **Personal settings**.
3. On the left, click **Notifications**.
4. Under **Topic notifications**, select the desired option from the **Automatically follow topics based on my participation** dropdown.

### Follow topics where you are mentioned

You can automatically follow topics where you are personally mentioned. If this setting is enabled:

- You will automatically follow topics where you are personally mentioned, but
group mentions and wildcard mentions (**@all**, **@topic**, etc.) will not
affect topic status.
- You will automatically follow topics in muted channels when you are mentioned.
- Mentions will *not* cause you to automatically follow topics you have
explicitly muted.

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select  **Personal settings**.
3. On the left, click **Notifications**.
4. Under **Topic notifications**, toggle **Automatically follow topics where I’m
mentioned**.

## Manage configured topics

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select  **Personal settings**.
3. On the left, click **Topics**.
4. Configure notifications for each topic by selecting the desired option from
the dropdown in the **Status** column.

**Mobile:**

1. Tap the **channels** () tab at the bottom of the app.
2. Select a channel containing topics you want to configure.
3. Tap **topics** ()
in the upper right corner of the app.
4. Press and hold a topic until the long-press menu appears.
5. Tap **Mute topic**, **Unmute topic**, or **Follow topic**.
