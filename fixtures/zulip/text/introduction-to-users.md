# Introduction to users

A **user** is an individual’s account within a Zulip
organization. Administrators can
configure how accounts are created in their
organization, and how users will log
in.

Zulip lets users and organization administrators configure the following details. This information is summarized in a user’s card, and presented in detail in their profile.

- Profile picture
- Name
- Role in the organization
- Status and availability, and whether the account has been deactivated
- Current local time
- Email address, with configurable permissions to view it
- Custom profile fields

Users can also be members of groups, and subscribe to channels.
