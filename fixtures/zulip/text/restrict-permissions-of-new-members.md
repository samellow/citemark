# Restrict permissions of new members

**Note:**

This feature is only available to organization owners.

In large Zulip organizations where anyone can join, it can be useful to restrict what new members can do, to make it easier to cope with spammers and confused users.

Members are **new members** from the moment they join until their account
ages beyond a certain **waiting period** threshold. After that, they are
**full members**. You can configure how long the waiting period is.

Zulip supports restricting access to certain features only to full members. Such features include, but are not limited to, creating channels, inviting users to the organization, and adding custom emoji.

 **Tip:** The **full members** role only appears as an option
for permission settings after a waiting period for new members has
been set.

## Set waiting period for new members

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select  **Organization settings**.
3. On the left, click **Organization permissions**.
4. Under **Joining the organization**, configure **Waiting period before new members turn into full members**.
5. Click **Save changes**.
