# User cards

In the web and desktop apps, user cards contain basic information about a user or bot. User cards are also a handy starting point for actions such as a viewing a user’s profile or viewing the messages they’ve sent.

Here is an overview of all the information and actions you can take on a user card.

## View someone’s user card

**User:**

1. Hover over a user’s name in the right sidebar.
2. Click on their avatar or the **ellipsis** () to the right of their name to open their **user card**.

 **Tip:** You can also click on a user’s profile picture or name on a message they sent.

 **Keyboard tip:** Alternatively, open someone’s **user card** by selecting a message they sent, and
using the `U` shortcut.

**Bot:**

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select  **Organization settings**.
3. On the left, click **Bots**.
4. Click on the name of a bot in the **Name** column to open its **user card**.

## Information in a user’s card

**User:**

- Their profile picture.
- Their name.
- Their role in the organization.
- Their status and availability, and whether the account has been deactivated.
- Their current local time.
- Their email address, if you have permission to view it.
- The custom profile fields selected by administrators to be featured on user cards.

**Bot:**

- Its profile picture.
- Its name.
- Its owner’s name.
- Its email address, if you have permission to view it.
- Whether the bot has been deactivated.
