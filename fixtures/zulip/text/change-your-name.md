# Change your name

By default, everyone can change their name in Zulip. You can write your name in any language you’d like.

Organization administrators can change anyone’s name, and prevent users from changing their names. This is useful when users’ names are managed via LDAP or another data source.

### Change your name

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select  **Personal settings**.
3. On the left, click **Profile**.
4. Edit the **Name** field.

**Note:** If you are unable to edit the name field, check
that your organization allows name changes. Follow the steps at
prevent users from changing their names
to view your organization’s settings.
