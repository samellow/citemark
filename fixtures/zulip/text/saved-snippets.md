# Saved snippets

You can save snippets of message content, and quickly insert them into the message you’re composing.

## Insert a saved snippet

1. Open the compose box.
2. Click the **add saved snippet** () icon at the
bottom of the compose box to open the saved snippets menu.
3. Start typing to filter saved snippets by title. Select a saved snippet to insert it into the compose box.

 **Keyboard tip:** You can also use `Ctrl` + `’` to open the saved
snippets menu.

## Create a saved snippet

1. Open the compose box.
2. *(optional)* Write the text you’d like to use as save as a snippet in the
compose box. You can preview what
it will look like once sent.
3. Click the **add saved snippet** () icon at the bottom
of the compose box to open the saved snippets menu.
4. Select **Create a new saved snippet**.
5. Enter a **Title** for the saved snippet, which will be used for finding saved
snippets.
6. Enter the **Content** you’d like to save as a snippet, or modify the content
from the compose box as needed.
7. Click **Save**.

 **Keyboard tip:** You can also use `Ctrl` + `’` to open the saved
snippets menu.

## Edit a saved snippet

1. Open the compose box.
2. Click the **add saved snippet** () icon at the bottom
of the compose box to open the saved snippets menu.
3. Hover over the saved snippet you’d like to edit, and click the **edit snippet** () icon on the right.
4. Edit the **Title** and **Content** as desired.
5. Click **Save**.

 **Keyboard tip:** You can also use `Ctrl` + `’` to open the saved
snippets menu.

## Delete a saved snippet

1. Open the compose box.
2. Click the **add saved snippet** () icon at the bottom
of the compose box to open the saved snippets menu.
3. Hover over the saved snippet you’d like to delete, and click the **delete
snippet** () icon on the right.

 **Keyboard tip:** You can also use `Ctrl` + `’` to open the saved
snippets menu.
