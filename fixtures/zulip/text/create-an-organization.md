# Create an organization

It only takes a minute to create a new organization on Zulip Cloud, or on a self-hosted Zulip server you’ve set up.

You can try out Zulip by creating a demo organization – no email required when you register. Explore the app, and invite others to check it out with you.

## Create a demo organization

**Note:**

This feature is only available on Zulip Cloud.

Demo organizations are automatically deleted after 30 days. If you’d like to preserve your message history, you can convert a demo organization into a regular organization.

1. Fill out a brief form, and click **Try Zulip**.

## Create a regular organization

**Zulip Cloud:**

**Note:**

To import data from another app, follow the import instructions for Slack, Mattermost or Rocket.Chat.

1. Decide on a name and subdomain for your organization.
2. Fill out the form at https://zulip.com/new/, and click **Create organization**.
3. Click **Complete registration** in the confirmation email you received.
4. Fill out your account information, and click **Sign up**.

**Self hosting:**

1. Follow the guide to install your server.
2. Decide on a name and subdomain for your organization.
3. Fill out the form at the URL provided by the installation script, and click **Create organization**.
4. Fill out your account information, and click **Sign up**.
