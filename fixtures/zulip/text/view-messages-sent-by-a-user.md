# View messages sent by a user

Zulip offers a convenient feature to search all messages sent by any user including yourself.

## View messages sent by a user

1. Hover over a user’s name in the right sidebar.
2. Click on their avatar or the **ellipsis** () to the right of their name to open their **user card**.
3. Select **View messages sent** to view all messages sent by this user.

## View messages you’ve sent

1. Hover over your name in the right sidebar.
2. Click on the **ellipsis** ()
to open your **user card**.
3. Select **View messages sent** to view all the messages you’ve sent.

 **Tip:** You can also search your messages by typing `sender:me` in the
search bar at the top of the app and pressing `Enter`.

## View direct messages with yourself

1. Hover over your name in the right sidebar.
2. Click on the **ellipsis** ()
to open your **user card**.
3. Select **View messages with yourself** to view messages you sent to
yourself.
