# Star a message

Starring messages is a good way to keep track of important messages, such as tasks you need to go back to or documents you reference often.

## Star a message

Starred messages have a filled in star () to their right.

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Hover over a message to reveal three icons on the right.
2. Click the **star** () icon.

 **Tip:** You can unstar a message using the same instructions used to star it.

 **Keyboard tip:** You can use `Ctrl` + `S` to star or unstar the selected
message.

**Mobile:**

1. Press and hold a message until the long-press menu appears.
2. Tap **Star message**.

 **Tip:** You can unstar a message by tapping **Unstar message** in the
long-press menu.

## View your starred messages

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Click on  **Starred messages** (or  if the **views** section is collapsed) in the left sidebar.

 **Tip:** You can also search your starred messages
using the `is:starred` filter.

 **Keyboard tip:** Use `*` to view your starred messages.

**Mobile:**

1. Tap the **menu** () tab in the bottom right corner of the app.
2. Tap  **Starred messages**.

## Unstar all messages in a topic

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Hover over a topic in the left sidebar.
2. Click on the **ellipsis** ().
3. Click **Unstar all messages in topic**. If you don’t see this option,
then you have no starred messages in the selected topic.
4. Approve by clicking **Confirm**.

## Unstar all messages

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Hover over  **Starred messages** in the **views** section of the left sidebar.
2. Click on the **ellipsis** ().
3. Click **Unstar all messages**. If you don’t see this option,
then you have no starred messages.
4. Approve by clicking **Confirm**.

 **Tip:** If the **views** section in the left sidebar is collapsed, click the
triangle to the left of **views** to uncollapse it.

## Toggle starred messages counter

By default, Zulip displays the number of starred messages in the left sidebar; this allows you to use them as an inbox of messages you’d like to come back to. If you are using starred messages for something else and would prefer not to see the count in your left sidebar, you can disable that feature.

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select  **Personal settings**.
3. On the left, click **Preferences**.
4. Under **Left sidebar**, toggle **Show counts for starred messages**.
