# Draft messages

Zulip automatically saves the content of your message as a draft when you close the compose box, ensuring that you never lose your work. When you start composing, the most recently edited draft for the conversation you are composing to automatically appears in the compose box.

**Note**: Drafts are local to your client, and are not synced to
other devices and browsers.

## Save a draft

### Save a draft and stop composing

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Open the compose box.
2. Type at least 3 characters of text into the compose box.
3. Close the compose box. For example, you can click the
**close** () icon in the top right
corner of the compose box, or click on an empty area of the app to close the
compose box and save your message as a draft.

 **Keyboard tip:** You can also use `Esc` to close the compose box and save your
message as a draft.

### Save a draft and start a new message

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Open the compose box.
2. Click the **ellipsis** () next to the **send** () button.
3. Select **Save draft and start a new message**.

## Restore a draft

To make it easier to find the draft you are looking for, drafts for the
conversation you are composing to are shown at the top of the drafts list in the
web and desktop apps. If you have saved drafts for the current conversation, the
counter next to the **Drafts** button in the compose box shows how many there are.

**Via left sidebar:**

1. Click on  **Drafts** in the left sidebar. If the **views** section is collapsed, click on
the **ellipsis** (),
and select **Drafts**.
2. Click on the draft you want to restore.

 **Keyboard tip:** You can also use `D` to bring up your list of saved drafts, and
`Enter` within the drafts view to restore the selected draft.

**Via compose box button:**

1. Open the compose box.
2. Click the **Drafts** button on the right side of the compose box.
3. Click on the draft you want to restore.

 **Keyboard tip:** You can also use `D` to bring up your list of saved drafts, and
`Enter` within the drafts view to restore the selected draft.

## Delete drafts

### Delete a draft

**Via left sidebar:**

1. Click on  **Drafts** in the left sidebar. If the **views** section is collapsed, click on
the **ellipsis** (),
and select **Drafts**.
2. Click the **delete** () icon on the draft you
want to delete.

 **Keyboard tip:** You can also use `D` to bring up your list of saved drafts, and
`Backspace` within the drafts view to delete the selected draft.

**Via compose box button:**

1. Open the compose box.
2. Click the **Drafts** button on the right side of the compose box.
3. Click the **delete** () icon on the draft you
want to delete.

 **Keyboard tip:** You can also use `D` to bring up your list of saved drafts, and
`Backspace` within the drafts view to delete the selected draft.

### Delete multiple drafts

**Via left sidebar:**

1. Click on  **Drafts** in the left sidebar. If the **views** section is collapsed, click on
the **ellipsis** (),
and select **Drafts**.
2. Click **Select all drafts** in the upper right corner of
the drafts view, or select the drafts you want to delete
by toggling the checkboxes on the right.
3. Click the **delete** () icon in the
upper right corner of the drafts view to delete all selected drafts.

 **Keyboard tip:** You can also use `D` to bring up your list of saved drafts.

**Via compose box button:**

1. Open the compose box.
2. Click the **Drafts** button on the right side of the compose box.
3. Click **Select all drafts** in the upper right corner of
the drafts view, or select the drafts you want to delete
by toggling the checkboxes on the right.
4. Click the **delete** () icon in the
upper right corner of the drafts view to delete all selected drafts.

 **Keyboard tip:** You can also use `D` to bring up your list of saved drafts.
