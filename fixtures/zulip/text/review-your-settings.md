# Review your settings

We recommend reviewing all of your settings when you start using Zulip, and then once again a few weeks later once you’ve gotten a better feel for how you use Zulip.

## Review your settings

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select **Personal Settings**.
3. Click on each tab on the left.

## Review your preferences

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select **Personal Settings**.
3. Click on the **Preferences** tab on the left.

## Review your privacy settings

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select **Personal Settings**.
3. Click on the **Account & privacy** tab on the left.

## Review your notification settings

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select **Personal Settings**.
3. Click on the **Notifications** tab on the left.
