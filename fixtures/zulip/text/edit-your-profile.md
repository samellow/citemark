# Edit your profile

By default, your profile includes your name, email, the date you joined, and when you were last active.

Organization administrators can also add custom profile fields. Custom profile fields are always optional, and will not appear in your profile unless you fill them out.

### Edit your profile

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select  **Personal settings**.
3. On the left, click **Profile**.
4. Edit the fields under **Profile**.
