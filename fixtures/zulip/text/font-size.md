# Font size

Zulip offers a range of font size options, from 12 to 20, to make the UI feel comfortable on any screen.

## Change font size

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Click on your **profile picture** in the upper right
corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Click to increase the font size, to decrease it, and to reset to the default.

## Zoom in or out

You can further adjust the font size by zooming in or out in your browser, or in the Zulip desktop app.

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Use `Ctrl` + `+` to zoom in, `Ctrl` + `-` to zoom out, or `Ctrl` + `0` to reset to default zoom.

 **Tip:** In the Zulip desktop app and most browsers, you can also open the **View**
menu in the top menu bar, and click **Zoom In**, **Zoom Out**, or **Actual
Size**.
