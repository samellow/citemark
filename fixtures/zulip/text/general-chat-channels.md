# “General chat” channels

Zulip’s topics help you keep conversations organized, but you may not need topics in some channels (e.g., a social channel, or one with a narrow purpose).

If you have permission to administer a channel, you can configure it to only
have the special “*general chat*” topic. The name of this topic is shown in
italics, and is translated into your language.

Users won’t need to enter a topic when sending a message to a “*general chat*”
channel.

 **Tip:** You can also allow topics, but configure who can start new
ones.

## Configure a channel to have only the “*general chat*” topic

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select  **Channel settings**.
3. Click **All** in the upper left.
4. Select a channel.
5. Select the **Permissions** tab on the right.
6. Under **Messaging permissions**, set **Allow posting to the *general chat*
topic?** to **Only “general chat” topic allowed**.
7. Click **Save changes**.

 **Tip:** You can also click on a channel name in the navigation bar at the top of the
app to access its settings, or use the **ellipsis** ()
next to the channel name in the left sidebar.
