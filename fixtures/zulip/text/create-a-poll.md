# Polls

Zulip makes it easy to create a poll. Polls in Zulip are collaborative, so anyone can add new options to a poll. However, only the creator of the poll can edit the question.

## Create a poll

**Via compose box button:**

1. Open the compose box.
2. Make sure the compose box is empty.
3. Click the **add poll** () icon at the bottom of the compose box.
4. Fill out poll information as desired, and click **Add poll** to insert poll
formatting.
5. Click the **send** () button, or use a keyboard
shortcut to send your message.

 **Tip:** To reorder the list of options, click and drag the **vertical dots**
() to the left of each
option. To delete an option, click the **delete**
() icon to the right of it.

**Via Markdown:**

1. Open the compose box.
2. Make sure the compose box is empty.
3. Type `/poll` followed by a space, and the question you want to ask.
4. *(optional)* Type each option on a new line.
5. Click the **send** () button, or use a keyboard
shortcut to send your message.

 **Tip:** You will be able to add options after the poll is created.

## Add options to a poll

**Note:**

To preserve the meaning of votes in the poll, existing poll options cannot be modified.

1. Fill out the **New option** field at the bottom of the poll.
2. Click **Add option** or press `Enter` to add the new option to
the poll.

## Edit the question

**Note:**

Only the creator of a poll can edit the question.

1. Click the **pencil** () icon
to the right of the question.
2. Edit the question as desired.
3. Click the **checkmark** () icon or press `Enter` to save your changes.

 **Tip:** You can click the  icon or press
`Esc` to discard your changes.

## Examples

### What you type

```
/poll What did you drink this morning?
Milk
Tea
Coffee
```
