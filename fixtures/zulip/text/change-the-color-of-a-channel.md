# Change the color of a channel

Zulip assigns each of your channels a color when you subscribe to the channel. Changing a channel’s color does not change it for anyone else.

**Via left sidebar:**

1. Hover over a channel in the left sidebar.
2. Click on the **ellipsis** ().
3. Click **Change color**.
4. Select a color from the grid, use the color picker, or enter a hex code.
5. Click **Confirm** to save and apply the color change.
6. Click outside the box to close the menu.

**Via channel settings:**

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select **Channel settings**.
3. Select a channel.
4. Select the **Personal** tab on the right.
5. Under **Personal settings**, click on the colored square below **Channel color**.
6. Select a color from the grid, use the color picker, or enter a hex code.
7. Click **Choose** to save and apply the color change.

 **Tip:** You can also click on a channel name in the navigation bar at the top of the
app to access its settings, or use the **ellipsis** ()
next to the channel name in the left sidebar.
