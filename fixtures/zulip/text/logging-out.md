# Logging out

## Log out of a Zulip account on one device

**Desktop/Web:**

1. Click the **gear** () icon in the top
right corner of the app.
2. Click **Log out**.

**Mobile:**

1. Tap the **menu** () tab in the bottom right corner of the app.
2. Tap the **switch account** () button.
3. Tap the **ellipsis** ()
to the right of the Zulip organization you want to log out of.
4. Tap **Log out**.
5. Approve by tapping **Log out**.

 **Tip:** Logging out doesn’t affect any other organizations you may be logged in
to. To log out of all your Zulip organizations, repeat the steps above
for each one.

## Log out of a Zulip account on all devices

Resetting the API key for a Zulip account will immediately log you out of that account on all mobile devices. Resetting your password will log you out of all browsers and desktop apps.

### Change your password and API key

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select  **Personal settings**.
3. On the left, click **Account & privacy**.
4. Under **Account**, click **Change your password**.
5. Enter your old password and your new password, and click **Change**.
6. Under **API key**, click **Manage your API key**.
7. Enter your new password, and click **Get API key**.
8. Click **Generate new API key**.

 **Tip:** If you’ve forgotten your password, you can reset
it
using your email account.
