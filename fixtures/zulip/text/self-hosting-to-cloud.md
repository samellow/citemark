# Move from self-hosting to Zulip Cloud

If you no longer want to manage your own server, you can always move your organization to Zulip Cloud.

## Move from self-hosting to Zulip Cloud

1. Export your organization’s data with user consent, or follow these instructions to get a full export without member consent.
2. Email support@zulip.com with the following
information:
  - The subdomain you would like to use for your organization. Your Zulip chat will
be hosted at `<subdomain>.zulipchat.com`.
  - Your **exported data** file (`.tar.gz` format).
