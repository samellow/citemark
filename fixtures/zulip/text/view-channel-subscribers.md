# View channel subscribers

Who is subscribed to a channel determines who receives the messages sent there. All non-guest users can view public channels and subscribe themselves. Organization administrators can configure who can subscribe and unsubscribe other users to channels.

## View channel subscribers

**Via channel settings:**

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select  **Channel settings**.
3. Click **All** in the upper left.
4. Select a channel.
5. Select the **Subscribers** tab on the right.

 **Tip:** You can also click on a channel name in the navigation bar at the top of the
app to access its settings, or use the **ellipsis** ()
next to the channel name in the left sidebar.

**Via left sidebar:**

1. Click on a channel in the left sidebar.
2. View subscribers in the **In this channel** section in the right sidebar. If
the section is collapsed, click **In this channel** to reveal it.

 **Tip:** To see the full list of subscribers for a channel that has more than 600
people subscribed, scroll to the bottom of the **In this channel** section,
and click **View all subscribers**.
