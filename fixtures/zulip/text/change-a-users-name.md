# Change a user's name

**Note:**

This feature is only available to organization owners and administrators.

By default, users can change their name, though organization administrators can restrict name changes.

Organization administrators can always change any user’s name.

## Change a user’s name

**Via user profile:**

1. Hover over a user’s name in the right sidebar.
2. Click on their avatar or the **ellipsis** () to the right of their name to open their **user card**.
3. Click on the **ellipsis** () in the user card.
4. Click **Manage this user**.
5. Under **Name**, enter a new name.
6. Click **Save changes**.

 **Tip:** You can also access the **Manage user** tab by clicking the **pencil and
paper** () icon at the top of the user
profile.

**Via organization settings:**

1. Click on the **gear** () icon in the upper right corner of the web or desktop app.
2. Select  **Organization settings**.
3. On the left, click **Users**.
4. Find the user you would like to manage. Click the
**manage user** () icon
to the right of their name.
5. Under **Name**, enter a new name.
6. Click **Save changes**.
