# Switching between organizations

This article assumes you’ve logged in to each organization at least once.

**Mobile:**

1. Tap the **menu** () tab in the bottom right corner of the app.
2. Tap the **switch account** () button.
3. Tap on the desired Zulip organization.

**Desktop:**

1. Click on a logo in the **organizations sidebar** on the left, or choose
an organization from the **Window** menu in the top menu bar.

 **Tip:** To show or hide the **organizations sidebar** on the left, select
**Toggle Sidebar** from the **View** menu in the top menu bar.

**Web:**

You can log in to multiple Zulip organizations by opening multiple tabs, and logging into one Zulip organization in each tab. To switch Zulip organizations, just switch tabs.
